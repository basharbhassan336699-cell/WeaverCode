Set up GitHub connection (one time)

    

      To enable the one-tap "Authorize" button, enter your own OAuth app details (stored on your device only).

      1) Open github.com/settings/developers → New OAuth App.

      2) Callback URL: http://localhost:8080/oauth/callback — and enable Device Flow (optional).

      3) Copy the Client ID, generate a Client Secret, and paste them here.
